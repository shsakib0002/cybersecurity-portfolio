Research & Publications

My research focuses on applying machine learning to radio-frequency signal analysis, with emphasis on robust modulation recognition under varying signal-to-noise ratios and channel conditions.

FEATURED THESIS · IN PROGRESS

RF Signal Classification and Modulation Recognition using CNN–LSTM Hybrid Deep Learning Models for Defense and Security Applications

Academic ProgramMaster of Information Systems Security (MISS)
InstitutionBangladesh University of Professionals (BUP)
SupervisorDr. Md. Zulfiker Mahmud
Professor, Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Jagannath University, Dhaka
StatusMaster’s Thesis — In Progress

This research investigates deep learning approaches for automatic modulation classification of RF signals using complex I/Q representations. The study focuses on model performance under different signal-to-noise ratios, channel conditions, and cross-dataset distribution shifts.

CNN–LSTM architectures are evaluated against simpler baselines to examine the trade-off between classification performance, robustness, and inference efficiency.
