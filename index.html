About Me

Telecom and infrastructure operations

In my current role at Huawei Technologies Bangladesh Ltd., I support the Grameenphone GP UDM project. My work includes monitoring Huawei 5G Core UDM and supporting infrastructure, analysing alarms and operational KPIs, performing initial troubleshooting, and coordinating escalation and service-restoration updates.

Previously, I worked in RF and network engineering, supporting wireless links through RF planning, link budgeting, spectrum analysis, site surveys, and PTP/PTMP deployment activities. I am pursuing an MSc in Information Systems Security (MISS) at BUP and continuing to build capability in Linux, cloud infrastructure, automation, and security operations.
