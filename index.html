About

From the radio link to the core network

I started in enterprise IT and e-commerce systems support, moved into RF and wireless link engineering, and now work on the core of a mobile network. That path gives me a practical view of how a service fails — and where to look first.

Today I work in the Grameenphone SOC for Huawei, where the job is simple to describe and demanding to do well: detect early, diagnose accurately, escalate clearly, and keep everyone informed until service is restored.

How an incident moves through my shift
  1. MonitorDashboards, alarms and KPIs in iMaster MAE
  2. AnalyseAlarm correlation and initial troubleshooting
  3. EscalatePer defined operational and escalation procedures
  4. CoordinateHuawei, Grameenphone and engineering teams through restoration
  5. Report & hand overIncident updates, KPI summaries, shift-handover notes
